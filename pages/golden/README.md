# Golden demo examples

Fetched 2026-10-22 with curl. Quotes are checked byte-for-byte against the saved HTML (tags stripped, whitespace collapsed). Ranked best live demo first.

| Rank | Company | URL | Saved file | Worst clause (verbatim) | Why it is bad | Regulator or press source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SiriusXM | https://www.siriusxm.com/customer-agreement | siriusxm.html | “You must cancel at least 24 hours before your upcoming renewal date to avoid being automatically renewed and charged for a new Subscription Period.”<br><br>“All Subscriptions may be cancelled by phone or online chat as follows:”<br><br>“no refunds or credits will be given for any partial period” | Cancelling needs a phone call or chat within set hours, 24 hours before renewal, and a cancelled plan is never refunded for the current period. | NY AG sued 2023-12-20: https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2023/attorney-general-james-sues-siriusxm-radio-trapping-consumers-unwanted ; court ruled SiriusXM broke the law with a burdensome cancellation process, 2024-11-22: https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2024/attorney-general-james-stops-siriusxm-trapping-new-york-customers-unwanted |
| 2 | Peloton | https://www.onepeloton.com/membership-terms | peloton.html | “The Committed Membership Period shall be considered the full billing cycle for your Peloton Membership, for which you are liable even if you pay the fee on a month-to-month basis.”<br><br>“You will remain responsible for any outstanding fees for the remainder of the Committed Membership Period following any cancellation.” | On a committed plan you still owe every remaining month after you cancel, even though you pay monthly. | None verified |
| 3 | AT&T | https://www.att.com/legal/terms.consumerServiceAgreement.html | att.html | “If you do not meet that commitment, you agree to pay an early termination fee.”<br><br>“if a term commitment to maintain service or programming for a particular length of time is not met, and either you cancel an AT&T Service or we terminate it for misconduct, you will be subject to any applicable early-termination fee(s)” | Leaving before the commitment ends costs an early termination fee, and the page does not state the amount. | None verified |
| 4 | Blue Apron | https://www.blueapron.com/pages/terms | blueapron.html | “ANY AUTOSHIP ORDER(S) IDENTIFIED AS “PROCESSING” OR “SHIPPED” ON YOUR UPCOMING PAGE, HOWEVER, CANNOT BE CANCELLED.”<br><br>“TO AVOID BEING CHARGED FOR THE NEXT MONTHLY OR ANNUAL PERIOD, YOU MUST CANCEL YOUR BLUE APRON+ SUBSCRIPTION PRIOR TO YOUR SCHEDULED BILLING DATE.” | Once a box is processing you cannot cancel it and you pay for it, and a missed billing date means another full period. | None verified |
| 5 | Audible | https://www.audible.com/conditions-of-use | audible.html | “If you cancel your membership or subscription, you will not receive a refund of any fees already paid.”<br><br>“Credits expire immediately upon the cancellation or termination of your membership unless used prior to cancellation or termination.” | Cancelling forfeits unused credits at once and returns no money already paid. | None verified |

Left out: Adobe, Amazon, Planet Fitness, LA Fitness, Spectrum, T-Mobile, Sling (pages blocked, 403 or timeout, or JS-only). Tinder and Match: the saved Tinder page showed no clause bad enough, and the FTC Match settlement ($14M, 2025-08-12, https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/08/match-group-agrees-pay-14-million-permanently-stop-deceptive-advertising-cancellation-billing) covers Match.com, not Tinder. NYT, WSJ, HelloFresh returned JS shells or 404.
